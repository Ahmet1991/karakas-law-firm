Privacy Notice

How personal data submitted through this website is processed under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698.

Draft text. This notice has been prepared to match the data the contact form actually collects. It should be reviewed by Att. Pınar Karakaş before launch, and the data controller title, retention periods and application address confirmed.

Data controller

Your personal data is processed by Karakaş Law Firm (Att. Pınar Karakaş) as data controller under Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), within the framework described below.

Address: Akdeniz Mah. 1353 Sk. No:2, Armesa İş Merkezi D:32, Konak / İzmir, Türkiye
Email: avpinarkarakas@gmail.com

Personal data processed

This website processes data only through the contact form, and only what you choose to provide:

  • Identity: your name and surname
  • Contact: your email address and, optionally, your telephone number
  • Enquiry content: the subject heading you select and the information contained in your message

The site uses no visitor tracking, analytics software or advertising cookies, and places no cookies in your browser in order to function. The map loads from Google Maps only when you press the "Load map" button; unless you do so, no request is sent to Google.

Purposes of processing

  • Receiving and assessing your enquiry
  • Responding to you and, where appropriate, arranging an initial consultation
  • Meeting legal obligations and providing evidence in any dispute

The information you share is not used for marketing, is not sold to third parties, and is not used for profiling.

Legal basis

Your data is processed on the basis of the explicit consent you give in the form (KVKK art. 5/1) and the legitimate interest of the data controller in responding to your enquiry (KVKK art. 5/2-f).

Transfers

Form submissions are delivered through a technical service provider and reach the firm's email account. As these providers' servers may be located outside Türkiye, your data may be considered transferred abroad under KVKK art. 9. That transfer relies on the explicit consent you give by submitting the form. Otherwise, your data is not shared with third parties except legally authorised public authorities.

Retention

If your enquiry does not result in an engagement, your data is deleted within a reasonable period after the enquiry has been assessed. If an engagement is established, the retention obligations arising from the Attorneyship Law and related legislation apply.

Your rights

Under KVKK art. 11, by applying to the data controller you have the right to:

  • Learn whether your personal data is being processed,
  • Request information if it has been processed,
  • Learn the purpose of processing and whether the data is used accordingly,
  • Know the third parties to whom it is transferred, in Türkiye or abroad,
  • Request correction if it is incomplete or inaccurate,
  • Request its erasure or destruction,
  • Request that correction, erasure or destruction be notified to third parties to whom the data was transferred,
  • Object to a result reached solely through automated analysis that is to your detriment,
  • Claim compensation if you suffer loss because of unlawful processing.

You may send your requests to avpinarkarakas@gmail.com. Applications are concluded within thirty days at the latest.

Withdrawing consent

You may withdraw the explicit consent you have given at any time by sending a notice to the email address above. Withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing carried out before it.
