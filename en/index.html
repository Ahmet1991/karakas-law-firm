01 About

A practice that works from inside commercial life.

Karakaş Law Firm was founded in İzmir by Attorney Pınar Karakaş. The firm acts for companies and individuals across a broad field — from corporate and commercial law to dispute resolution, from maritime trade to healthcare, real estate, finance and employment law.

It provides ongoing counsel to companies in the healthcare, chemicals, marble, machinery, engineering, fuel and furniture sectors, and to hospital groups — including on international matters.

In disputes, our priority is to reach a commercially sustainable resolution before a matter reaches the courtroom. Through the firms we work with in other jurisdictions, we handle cross-border files with the same proximity.

Legal advice creates value only when it sits on the commercial reality of the business.

03 Founder

Pınar Karakaş

Founder · Attorney at Law

She graduated from İstanbul Bilgi University Faculty of Law and completed her master's degree in international commercial law at Coventry University in the United Kingdom with honours.

Following her work at a respected law firm in İstanbul and her role as counsel to a group of companies reporting directly to the Turkish Savings Deposit Insurance Fund (TMSF), she returned to İzmir and founded Karakaş Law Firm.

She currently advises and represents leading companies in the healthcare, chemicals, marble, machinery, engineering, fuel and furniture sectors, as well as hospital groups, including on international matters.
